Investment Philosophy

We invest with a multi-year horizon, concentrating capital where structural tailwinds, durable competitive advantages, and reasonable valuations converge.

High-conviction positions in businesses we understand deeply. Fewer names, greater conviction — not a broad index masquerading as a portfolio.

Operational alpha

Beyond passive capital, the office operates businesses directly — in fitness, e-commerce, and IP licensing — generating cash flows that compound the portfolio base.
